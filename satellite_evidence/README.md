# Recovered satellite evidence pilots

These two bounded Sentinel-2 change-review bundles were recovered from the predecessor combined
workspace because the current Data Center Atlas documentation still referenced them and no other
copy survived in the three split repositories. Their original 20 files remain byte-for-byte
unchanged and are protected by `tests/test_recovered_legacy_evidence.py`.

- `colossus-2-2024-2026/` reviews a known Colossus 2 campus area.
- `docklands-2024-2026/` reviews a known Ada Infrastructure Docklands parcel.

They are analyst-review aids, not a general data-centre detector. A spectral-change proposal is not
a site-identity, construction-status, floor-area, power, energy, or operating-status claim.
