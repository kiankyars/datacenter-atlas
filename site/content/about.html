What this is

{{site_name}} brings together data-centre construction projects from company disclosures, government records and regulatory filings. Each project includes a location, a dated construction status and links to its sources.

Explore the map, browse sites or countries, or download the data. Developers can use the JSON API. The atlas is maintained by Kian Kyars.

Corrections and suggestions

Found an error, or know of a construction project that qualifies? Use the correction form or the new-site form on GitHub. Every site page also has a "Report a correction" link that fills in the site ID. Please link public, primary sources, with dates.

Accepted additions and corrections appear in the next data update. Earlier files remain available in the archive. Submissions are checked against the selection rules.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it loads no scripts or fonts from other sites. When a map is shown, your browser requests basemap tiles directly from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile servers or from EOX (and from CARTO, where its maps are offered), which receive your IP address and the map area requested. The map remembers settings such as your basemap choice in your browser's local storage; they are not sent anywhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (GitHub's data-collection note). The OpenStreetMap, Google Maps, Bing Maps and Copernicus Browser links and the "Archived copy" links to the Internet Archive on site pages are only followed if you click them.

Source code and credits

The site, API and downloads are generated from the public repository kiankyars/datacenter-atlas by a standard-library Python builder, and the frontend has no build step. The data rests on public disclosures and open data from hundreds of publishers, listed on the attribution page, and on the work of OpenStreetMap contributors. Data updates are announced in the updates feed, and newly added sites in the sites feed.
